Prime-power structure of the stable regime for modular Schur numbers

Sm(k,ℓ) = m/gcd(m,ℓ−1) − 1 for all k ≥ n−1, with a Lean 4 formalization behind an independent comparator gate.

  • A closed form that holds for every modulus. With n = m/gcd(m,ℓ−1), we prove Sm(k,ℓ) = n − 1 for all m,ℓ ≥ 2 and k ≥ n−1. This settles the m ≥ 8 case left open by D’orville–Sim–Wong–Ho (2025) and subsumes the earlier per-modulus results. The proof is checked by the Lean 4 kernel against mathlib alone: no sorry, no project axioms, no native_decide.
  • The color threshold is exact at prime moduli, and composite moduli behave differently. For a prime p with ℓ ≥ p−1 and ℓ ≢ 1 (mod p), the threshold is k0(p,ℓ) = p − 1. In ℤ/12 the pair {1, 5} is a safe two‑element class, which refutes the natural guess that only singletons are safe.
  • The composite threshold reduces to one finite question, which stays open. In the stable regime the threshold depends only on ℓ mod m and splits as K + τ. We prove a coordinate‑union edge rule and a separated‑fiber criterion for the residual cover τ, but certified fibers (n = 880, and n ∈ {330, 660}) break the matching witness a uniform closed form would need, so τ at composite m remains conjectural.

Prove2.me missions

Missions I captain on Prove2.me, where every Lean 4 statement and proof is checked by the platform. Each link goes to the mission page, its milestones, and its verified theorems.

  • Modular Schur numbers: a uniform closed form in the stable-colour regime — the mission behind the paper above: Sm(k,ℓ) = m/gcd(m,ℓ−1) − 1 for every modulus in the many-colour regime proved
  • Near enemies: spherical sets project to minimal-energy images in general position — every finite set on a sphere has a generic plane projection that attains the Lund–Sheffer–de Zeeuw bisector-energy floor 2n(n−1) and lands in general position proved
  • Balog–Szemerédi–Gowers theorem over additive energy — the asymmetric qualitative form over an arbitrary additive commutative group, stated on Finset.addEnergy proved
  • Pach–de Zeeuw: finite Bézout bound for real plane curves — bounded-degree real plane curves with no common component meet in a bounded number of points proved
  • Superlinear or exact bounds for planar distinct distances — distinct distances grow superlinearly for planar sets with no three collinear and no four cocircular points open
  • Equational magmas: E677 → E255 (finite case) — does the E677 law force E255 on every finite magma? Open in the finite case open
  • Collatz Conjecture — the 3n+1 problem, decomposed into milestones open
  • Erdős Problems 97 and 96: convex point sets and unit distances — closed 13 September 2026: both questions are answered negatively by the convex construction of Kruer, Kohlmeyer and Price, so the affirmative package is disproved closed
